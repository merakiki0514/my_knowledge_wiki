# WireWatch: Measuring the security of proprietary network encryption in the global Android ecosystem

> Wang, Mona, Knockel, Jeffrey, Reichert, Zoe et al. — *, 0000*

## Summary

We present WireWatch, a large-scale measurement pipeline to evaluate the network security of Android apps. WireWatch measures apps’ usage of plaintext network traffic and non-standard, proprietary network cryptography. We found that 47.6% of top Mi Store applications used proprietary network cryptography without any additional encryption, compared to only 3.51% of top Google Play Store applications. We analyzed the 18 most popular protocols from WireWatch, which belonged to 9 protocol families, including cryptosystems designed by Alibaba, iQIYI, Kuaishou, and Tencent.
