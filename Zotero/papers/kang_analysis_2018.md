# Analysis of onboard ship cybersecurity

> Kang, Nam-Seon — *Journal of the Korean Society of Marine Engineering, 2018*

## Summary

In this paper, we analyze the status of ship cyber security and identify international trends in the cybersecurity of international maritime organizations and international standardization organizations. We analyze the guidelines on cyber security aboard ships, cyber-enabled ships (Lloyd's Register), and cyber security principles of marine and offshore operations (American Bureau of Shipping). This article discusses cyber security technologies in areas of awareness improvement, implementation procedure establishment, technologies, and guidelines education.
